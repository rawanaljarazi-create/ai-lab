Shellbox draws Fireworks Mania shells, meshes, and particles inside Garry's Mod.

The 3D picture is working. The inventory mirror is gone. Garry's Mod now has its own firework list. Hold Alt and click a name (or press 1-9) to spawn that firework on the crosshair and light it. A shell that hits the map or a prop stops, bursts, and the blast pushes nearby props.

The new guest plugin loads only when Fireworks Mania is restarted. In Garry's Mod, run `lua_openscript_cl autorun/client/shellbox.lua` so the list appears without a map change.

Play it single-player. Fireworks Mania can host public lobbies; start it without this mod before you join anyone.

Garry's Mod owns your body and the list. Fireworks Mania owns the shells. Your Garry's Mod health is not involved.
